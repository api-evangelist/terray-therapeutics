# Terray Therapeutics

> Terray Therapeutics is an AI-native, chemistry-first biotechnology company building an integrated
> full-stack platform for small molecule drug discovery. Its EMMI platform (Experimentation Meets
> Machine Intelligence) pairs a proprietary ultra-miniaturized microarray assay system and a fully
> automated lab with a family of machine learning models — COATI (a chemistry foundation model
> trained on ~1 billion molecules), reinforcement-learning generative models for synthesizable de
> novo design, TerraBind for potency prediction, and EMMI Select for uncertainty-aware molecule
> selection. Terray reports a proprietary target-molecule interaction dataset of more than 17
> billion measurements, growing by roughly 1 billion per quarter.

Terray publishes **no public developer API**, no developer portal, and no machine-readable API
contract (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, MCP server, or A2A agent card were found on any Terray
host as of 2026-08-02). Its public technical surface is open-source research code on GitHub.

## Open source

- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/terraytherapeutics): Terray Therapeutics on GitHub.
- [COATI](https://github.com/terraytherapeutics/COATI): Multi-modal contrastive pre-training for representing and traversing chemical space (Apache-2.0, Python).
- [COATI-LDM](https://github.com/terraytherapeutics/COATI-LDM): Latent diffusion for conditional generation of molecules (Python).
- [TerraMax](https://github.com/terraytherapeutics/terramax): Pretrained joint predictions for scalable batch Bayesian optimization of molecular designs (Apache-2.0, Python).
- [streamlit-ketcher (fork)](https://github.com/terraytherapeutics/streamlit-ketcher): Terray fork of the Ketcher molecule-editor Streamlit component.
